Why it's worth the walk

  • Roasted this week

    We roast on Mondays and Thursdays in small batches, so the bag you buy and the cup you drink are never more than ten days old.

  • A seat for the afternoon

    Free Wi-Fi, sockets at every table and nobody rushing you out. Bring a laptop, a book or a friend.

  • Neighbours behind the counter

    Our baristas remember your order, and our buns, loaves and tarts come from bakers within two miles.
